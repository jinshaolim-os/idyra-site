THE IDEA

Light at the centre.
Life around it.

The living, dining and kitchen spaces open to a planted courtyard. Timber slats filter the entrance. The rear wing holds the bedroom and bathroom; the open study sits beside the kitchen in the east wing.

An authored demonstration, not client work.

This is an original fictional residence with illustrative furniture and finishes. It is not a real property, as-built survey, approved building plan or construction-ready design. Controls demonstrate presentation choices, not building-performance simulation.
