WHAT THESE PICTURES ARE FOR

Concepts set the direction.
Your model does the explaining.

A concept image shows a possible look and feel. The deliverable is a digital 3D model of your actual building, with the views and illustrative layouts agreed in your quotation.

01

What we need from you

  • The type of space, its approximate size and what you want people to understand
  • Building-plan drawings as a PDF, or a scan if legible, sent after a signed order
  • Photographs or a brief for finishes and furnishing, when the look matters
  • Permission to use the plans and supplied content
02

What you receive

  • An interactive 3D model that opens in a browser, with agreed views and illustrative layouts
  • Presentation stills; a vertical video or film when your scope includes them
  • A facts page or spec sheet that cites its drawing sheets
  • Browser-ready files with handover instructions
03

Where it helps

  • Presenting a factory or warehouse to prospects before a visit
  • Discussing a layout or fit-out idea with your team
  • Showing a café, shop or office concept to partners and landlords
  • Explaining home ideas to family and your designer
04

What it is not

  • Not an as-built survey: the model follows the drawings
  • Not an engineering, process or compliance design
  • Not live monitoring or an operational simulation
  • Not a property listing: we do not sell or let property

FROM YOUR BUILDING TO YOUR PRESENTATION

Four steps.
Clear scope at each one.

  1. Free 20-minute review

    Tell us about your space. We explain what is possible, which inputs are needed and what your project would include.

  2. Written quotation and order

    The scope, deliverables, revisions, timeline and price are confirmed in writing. Nothing is payable before the order form is signed.

  3. Build and private preview

    After the signed order, send the authorised plans. We build the model, then work through the included revisions with you.

  4. Handover

    Receive the agreed files, ready to open and share. Hosting, access, term and upkeep are agreed separately.
