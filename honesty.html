Honesty and labellingPlan-checked by Idyra: what it means

Know what you are looking at.

A drawing-built model, an illustrative fit-out, AI-generated people, an AI-animated clip, an AI concept sample and an AI-generated presenter are different things. Every visual carries its source label, so you can tell them apart.

The drawing-built sample

One camera. Two labelled views.

The same day view of Demonstration project: as per plan and with a modelled fit-out. Demonstration project, not client work, not offered for sale or rent through Idyra.

Measured

Dimension graphics, plan overlays and tap-to-measure readouts computed from the model built from the drawings. Shown to 0.1 m and 1 m², never in centimetres. Not a survey.

Rendered from building-plan drawings

Path-traced or 3D output with no AI pixels. "Fit-out illustrative" is added whenever racking, goods, furniture, equipment or vehicles appear, because none of that is on the drawings.

Spec sheet · from building-plan drawings

A page, or part of one, from our spec sheet PDF. Every figure comes from the building-plan drawings and cites its sheet; derived, scaled and counted figures are marked as such, and where the drawings are silent it says "owner to confirm". Its key plan is redrawn from our model: the sheet is our document, not a copy of the drawings, and not a survey.

AI-illustrative

Any image with AI-generated pixels, even inside a mask. "People/ambience" is added when that is all the AI touched. AI-illustrative people are generated and do not show real individuals.

AI-animated

A short clip in which AI added motion to one of our drawing-built renders: camera movement, light, people or vehicles. The first frame is our render. The motion, people and vehicles are illustrative; measurements come from the drawings and the model, never from a clip.

Concept sample · AI-illustrative

An AI-generated image showing how another kind of property could be presented. Not a real building, not built from drawings and not client work.

AI-generated presenter

A fictional digital presenter with a synthetic voice, not an employee or customer. Presenter media is labelled and does not represent a customer testimonial.

The Demonstration project renders

The building stays in the model.

When we add AI people to a render, we use masks over non-structural areas. Walls, windows, doors, openings and dimensions remain unchanged. The current public drawing-built gallery contains native renders.

Masked, then composited

The model's output is used only inside the mask. Outside it, the composite matches the original render pixel for pixel; our compositing step checks this before the label is added and the image is compressed for the web.

Why the rule exists

On Demonstration project, all 20 raw outputs from the image model had repainted parts of the frame outside the mask. Unmasked, those changes would have reached the images.

Fit-out is modelled

Racking, forklifts and office furniture are 3D models placed in the to-scale building, not painted in by AI, and they are labelled illustrative.

AI-animated clips and concept samples

Illustration is not project evidence.

Our concept images explore possible presentations for industrial, commercial and home spaces. They are original AI-generated illustrations, not completed client projects, measured replicas or screenshots of an interactive model.

Working browser demonstrations are identified separately. A generated concept image does not promise the same detail or features in a browser model. Your quote defines the visual style, interactive features and outputs for your project.

The drawing-built demonstration film uses native camera moves through the model; its fit-out is illustrative. Videos play only when you choose.

Plan-checked by Idyra

What it means, and what it does not

Each project keeps a source record. Public examples may use an anonymous drawing reference. This is the line that accompanies the model:

Model geometry checked by Idyra against building-plan drawing set [reference] ([date]; [approval status]). Furnishing, equipment and people are illustrative (modelled or AI-visualised, as labelled). Not as-built, not a survey, and not a certification or approval by any authority, architect or engineer.

On Demonstration project it reads: drawing set Demonstration drawing set (JUN 2026; submitted, not yet approved).

It means

We read the drawings into a to-scale model and checked the model back against them. On Demonstration project, 20 of 20 random dimensions came within 0.1 m; all 103 checks passed (101 dimensions, 2 counts). Where the drawings are silent, we say "owner to confirm".

It does not mean

That the building matches its drawings, that the drawings have received approval, or that a use is permitted. Idyra is not an authority, an architect or an engineer. For decisions, verify on site and with the title and planning documents.

Drawings and data

What happens to what you send us

Drawings stay private

We never publish drawing sheets or their title blocks. Showrooms carry our model and renders, never the drawing files. We delete them on request.

A written licence

Before we build a client's showroom, whoever owns the copyright in the drawings (often the architect, the engineer or the company that commissioned them) licenses us in writing, and the registered owner authorises the showroom in writing.

Personal data

The quote builder prepares a message in your browser. Opening WhatsApp passes it the draft; we receive it only if you send it. The separate partner application securely submits and stores your details for private review when you choose that option. Browser preferences, provider processing and retention are explained in our BM/EN privacy notice.
