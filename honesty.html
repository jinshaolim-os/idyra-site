Know what you are looking at.

A drawing-built model, an illustrative fit-out, AI-generated people, an AI-animated clip, an AI concept sample and an AI-generated presenter are different things. Every visual carries its source label, so you can tell them apart.

The drawing-built sample

One camera. Three labelled views.

The same day view of PT 49665: as per plan, with a modelled fit-out, and with AI people. PT 49665 is our founder's family property, not a paying client, and it is not offered for sale or rent through Idyra.

Measured

Dimension graphics, plan overlays and tap-to-measure readouts computed from the model built from the drawings. Shown to 0.1 m and 1 m², never in centimetres. Not a survey.

Rendered from building-plan drawings

Path-traced or 3D output with no AI pixels. "Fit-out illustrative" is added whenever racking, goods, furniture, equipment or vehicles appear, because none of that is on the drawings.

Spec sheet · from building-plan drawings

A page, or part of one, from our spec sheet PDF. Every figure comes from the building-plan drawings and cites its sheet; derived, scaled and counted figures are marked as such, and where the drawings are silent it says "owner to confirm". Its key plan is redrawn from our model: the sheet is our document, not a copy of the drawings, and not a survey.

AI-illustrative

Any image with AI-generated pixels, even inside a mask. "People/ambience" is added when that is all the AI touched. AI-illustrative people are generated and do not show real individuals.

AI-animated

A short clip in which AI added motion to one of our drawing-built renders: camera movement, light, people or vehicles. The first frame is our render. The motion, people and vehicles are illustrative; measurements come from the drawings and the model, never from a clip.

Concept sample · AI-illustrative

An AI-generated image showing how another kind of property could be presented. Not a real building, not built from drawings and not client work.

AI-generated presenter

A computer-generated presenter, not a real person, speaking with a synthetic preset voice from an AI voice tool's library. We did not clone anyone's voice. Its spoken list of labels names only Measured, Rendered from building-plan drawings and AI-illustrative; otherwise it says only what this site says. The video has captions, and its transcript is under it in "Who we are" on our home page.

The PT 49665 renders

The building stays in the model.

In the AI-people versions of our drawing-built renders, we composite generated people only inside masks over non-structural areas. Walls, windows, doors, openings and dimensions remain unchanged.

Masked, then composited

The model's output is used only inside the mask. Outside it, the composite matches the original render pixel for pixel; our compositing step checks this before the label is added and the image is compressed for the web.

Why the rule exists

On PT 49665, all 20 raw outputs from the image model had repainted parts of the frame outside the mask. Unmasked, those changes would have reached the images.

Fit-out is modelled

Racking, forklifts and office furniture are 3D models placed in the to-scale building, not painted in by AI, and they are labelled illustrative.

AI-animated clips and concept samples

Illustration is not project evidence.

The looping clips on our home page, in the opening view and in the three property panels, are AI-animated. An image-to-video model started from our drawing-built renders of PT 49665 and added camera movement and ambience: light, people and vehicles. Each clip was checked against its source render, and clips that added or changed anything on the building were rejected. They are still illustrations, labelled AI-animated: use the drawing-built model and its measurements to inspect the geometry. With reduced motion or data saving switched on, the page shows the labelled render instead. The walkthrough film has no AI-animated shot.

The concept samples on our home page cover office towers, office suites in towers, retail units, condominiums, landed houses and mixed-use buildings.

Plan-checked by Idyra

What it means, and what it does not

Each showroom names the drawing set it was built from. This is the line that appears with it:

Model geometry checked by Idyra against building-plan drawing set [reference] ([date]; [approval status]). Furnishing, equipment and people are illustrative (modelled or AI-visualised, as labelled). Not as-built, not a survey, and not a certification or approval by any authority, architect or engineer.

On PT 49665 it reads: drawing set JPLKSB/49665-2026 (JUN 2026; submitted, not yet approved).

It means

We read the drawings into a to-scale model and checked the model back against them. On PT 49665, 20 of 20 random dimensions came within 0.1 m; all 103 checks passed (101 dimensions, 2 counts). Where the drawings are silent, we say "owner to confirm".

It does not mean

That the building matches its drawings, that the drawings have received approval, or that a use is permitted. Idyra is not an authority, an architect or an engineer. For decisions, verify on site and with the title and planning documents.

Drawings and data

What happens to what you send us

Drawings stay private

We never publish drawing sheets or their title blocks. Showrooms carry our model and renders, never the drawing files. We delete them on request.

A written licence

Before we build a client's showroom, whoever owns the copyright in the drawings (often the architect, the engineer or the company that commissioned them) licenses us in writing, and the registered owner authorises the showroom in writing. For our own case study, our founder's family property, these documents are being put in place.

Personal data

Our pages send nothing to us: no forms that submit data, no cookies and no analytics. The 3D model may remember a colour choice in your own browser, and the web host keeps standard access logs. If you message us on WhatsApp or by email, we use your message only to reply and to prepare what you asked for. See the privacy notice in Bahasa Melayu and English.
