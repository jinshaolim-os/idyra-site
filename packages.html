Terms in brief

How ordering works

Paperwork first

A written order form sets the scope, the drawing licence, the labelling rules and the acceptance criteria. No payment is taken before it is signed.

Payment

We confirm production availability and payment arrangements in your written order. Nothing is payable before the order form is signed. The order form sets out the payment terms, including any deposit and when it is refunded against the written criteria (dimensions, labels, number of deliverables, deadline).

Your drawings stay yours

We take a licence to use them for your showroom, never publish them, and delete them on request.

Prices

Prices are in Malaysian ringgit. Tax treatment will be shown on the order form. Ask about founding places in your scope review; nothing is payable until the order form is signed.

Project enquiries and quotations are open. We confirm production availability and payment arrangements in your written order. Nothing is payable before the order form is signed. Please don't send drawings before the order form is signed.

We never take a percentage of a sale or a tenancy.
