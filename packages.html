Terms in brief

How ordering works

Paperwork first

A written order form sets the scope, the drawing licence, the labelling rules and the acceptance criteria. No payment is taken before it is signed.

Payment

Orders open once our business bank account in the name of IDYRA DIGITALS STUDIO is open and our order paperwork is complete; until then we take no deposit or payment of any kind, and nothing is payable before the order form is signed. The order form sets out the payment terms, including any deposit and when it is refunded against the written criteria (dimensions, labels, number of deliverables, deadline).

Your drawings stay yours

We take a licence to use them for your showroom, never publish them, and delete them on request.

Prices

Prices are in Malaysian ringgit. Tax treatment will be shown on the order form. Ask about founding places on WhatsApp; nothing is payable until the order form is signed.
