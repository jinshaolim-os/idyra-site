Free project review

What to include

Whether you start in the app or by email, these details help us prepare your free 20-minute review.

See an example first.

Demos show working browser models; concepts show fictional visual ideas. These examples open in a new tab.

Café demo ↗Industrial demo ↗Concept gallery ↗

  1. Your space

    The type of space, its town or area and approximate floor size. A shop, café, office, factory or warehouse is a useful starting point.

  2. Your starting point

    Tell us whether building plans are available; the model is built from them. Photos or a phone video can help as reference. You do not need to send files for the first conversation. We agree the required inputs and permissions before production. How we handle what you send.

  3. Your goal

    Show a property, explain a business space or explore a layout. Tell us who will use the presentation and when you need it.

Please do not send identity-card numbers or bank details by email. We never ask for them by email.

What happens next

From brief to preview

A free 20-minute review

We discuss your space, audience and goal, show relevant labelled examples and explain what we need. The review and quotation are free; production is a paid service.

A clear written quote

Choose the deliverables that fit: a 3D presentation, still images, a video or layout options. We confirm the scope, required inputs, timing and price before you decide.

Agree, build and review

After the order form and required permissions are in place, we build your agreed presentation for review. Delivery, revisions and any hosting are set out in the order.
