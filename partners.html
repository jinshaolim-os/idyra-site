A partnership that fits.

We’re welcoming interest from people who know shops, cafés, offices, factories or homes. Tell us where you see a fit.

  1. Say hello

    Share a little about your work, your area and the kinds of spaces you know.

  2. Find the right fit

    We discuss your role, what the service can do and how a good introduction would work.

  3. Make it official

    When the pilot is ready, agree written terms before receiving a personal card and referral process.

The practical details

The proposed structure has no joining fee, required purchase or recruitment rewards. Any referral payment will be agreed in writing; income is not assured.

This concerns Idyra digital presentation services. Partners do not sell or let property, negotiate property transactions or collect client payments on Idyra’s behalf.

Commission terms and partner activation are still being finalised. Applying starts a conversation, not an appointment.
