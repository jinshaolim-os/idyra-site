For the people who sell and let factories and warehouses

Owners and landlords pay for the showroom. Registered estate agents bring it to the listing. Developers use it to show building types before they are built. Every figure carries its sheet reference, so the people deciding can check it before a site visit.

Owners and landlords

Show the empty unit working

Buyers looking at an empty shell have to imagine the rest. A showroom lets them see your building as drawn and the same building racked, with forklifts and a fitted office, on their phone, before they book a viewing.

Works before it can be filmed

Tenanted, still operating or not yet built: we work from the drawings, so none of that stops the showroom. It shows the building as drawn; whether it was built as drawn is for the owner to confirm.

Labelled, side by side

The as-per-plan view always sits beside the fit-out, and every visual says what it is.

Registered estate agentsREN · REA · PEA

Show the owner what a showroom does for their listing

At the listing meeting, show our sample showroom. After an order form is signed, with the owner's written go-ahead and a licence for the drawings, we then build theirs: empty as per plan and ready to operate, labelled.

The owner pays, your commission stays yours

The showroom goes into the marketing plan the owner approves, and Idyra invoices the owner. We never share in a commission and never take a percentage.

Your listing, your advertisement

A live share page is published as your firm's own advertisement, with your firm's name, E-number and office phone, your REN and the details the Board's rules require. Enquiries go to your WhatsApp; Idyra appears only as the supplier.

Answers to technical questions

Clear heights, bay doors, parking and areas, each with its sheet reference, in a spec sheet you can forward.
