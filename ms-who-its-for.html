Registered estate agentsREN · REA · PEA

Give the owner a clearer presentation option.

Introduce a digital presentation as part of the owner's marketing plan. We agree the scope and required permissions, then prepare the visuals for the registered agency's use.

The owner pays, your commission stays yours

The showroom goes into the marketing plan the owner approves, and Idyra invoices the owner. We never share in a commission and never take a percentage.

Your listing, your advertisement

A live share page is published as your firm's own advertisement, with your firm's name, E-number and office phone, your REN and the details the Board's rules require. Enquiries go to your WhatsApp; Idyra appears only as the supplier.

Useful information, in one place

Agree the views, key details and supporting material that help explain the property. Drawing-based facts remain traceable to their source.

Idyra supplies visual presentations; the registered agency handles the property listing and enquiries. For introductions to Idyra's services, see our referral partner programme.
