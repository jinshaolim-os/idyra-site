From drawings to a digital twin.

We work from the building-plan drawings, so the building does not need to be finished, empty or tidy. After an order form is signed, you send the drawings, check the preview and approve the result; the rest is our job.

The pipeline

Six stages, each checked before the next

  1. Quote and order

    Tell us about the building and whether you have the drawings. We quote from the fixed price list. Work starts once the order form is signed and the written licence for the drawings is in place.

  2. Drawings in

    After the order form is signed, you send the building-plan PDF. We confirm every sheet is there and readable, note the drawing reference, date and approval status, and ask the owner for a short written go-ahead. We never publish the drawings.

  3. Facts out of the sheets

    Every printed dimension, level, door, bay, area and schedule is read into a fact list with its sheet reference. A second pass re-reads the buyer-facing facts on the sheets. Where the drawings disagree with each other, we record it and ask. On PT 49665 this gave 1,835 facts, 195 of them buyer-facing and re-checked.

  4. The to-scale model

    The model is built from those facts, in metres, with the site, the building and its structure. Then it is checked back against the drawings: on PT 49665, all 103 checks passed (101 dimensions within 0.1 m and 2 counts exact), and 5 items that need a site visit were flagged.

  5. States and views

    The building as per plan, plus illustrative fit-outs such as logistics or light manufacturing. The model supports walking, state changes and tap-to-measure. A Showroom adds path-traced stills with as-per-plan partners and a vertical video; a longer film is an add-on. In the PT 49665 drawing-built renders, AI people are composited only inside non-structural masks and labelled AI-illustrative. Short AI-animated clips made from these renders appear on our home page with their own label.

  6. Review, then share

    You get a private preview and one round of revisions. Then the share page goes live with its WhatsApp preview card, the spec sheet PDF and the video files, ready for the listing.

What you get

The pieces of a showroom

Which pieces come with each package is on the packages page.

3D

Walk-in model

To scale, in the phone or desktop browser, with nothing to install. Switch between as per plan and the fit-out presets; tap two points to measure to 0.1 m (not a survey). The 3D model works better on a desktop; on a phone it can take 10–15 seconds to become interactive.

Stills

Path-traced stills

Rendered with Cycles at 1920 × 1080: exteriors by day and dusk, the loading bay, the hall, the office. Each operation-ready still has an as-per-plan partner.

Film

Film and vertical cuts

Camera moves through the model, the as-per-plan wipe and a screen capture of the 3D view. A Showroom includes one 20–30 s vertical video; the film add-on adds a 45–60 s walkthrough with 3 vertical cuts of 15 s.

PDF

Spec sheet

Areas, heights, doors, parking and finishes, each with its sheet reference. Where the drawings are silent, it says "owner to confirm".

Link

Share page

One page for the listing with a WhatsApp preview card, the agency's particulars and clear labels on every visual.

Not offered yet

In development: building-facts assistant

Answers questions about the building from the drawings, citing the sheet, in English, Bahasa Melayu and 中文. It is a pilot and not part of any package yet: 80% correct on 50 unseen questions against a 95% target, with no invented figures and no price talk.

What we need from you

Three things before we start

The drawings

After an order form is signed, the building-plan set as a PDF, all sheets. A scan works if it is legible. Tell us their approval status: the approval reference and date, or that they are only submitted.

The owner's go-ahead

A short written authority from the registered owner, and confirmation that we may use the drawings to make the showroom. We send you the wording.

What to show

Which operation-ready preset suits the buyers you want, and anything the drawings do not cover that the owner can confirm, such as power supply or floor loading.

Limits

What a showroom is not

  • Not as-built and not a survey. The model follows the drawings. For existing buildings, what was built can differ from the drawings. We're setting up an on-site check of key dimensions; until then, ask us before ordering.
  • Not an approval. We say which drawing set we used, its date and its approval status. Our check is of our model against those drawings, and is not a certification by any authority, architect or engineer.
  • Not a promise about use. Operation-ready presets are illustrative. Whether a use is allowed depends on the land title and planning approval.
  • Not an estate agency. We prepare showrooms. Registered estate agents and owners market the property.
