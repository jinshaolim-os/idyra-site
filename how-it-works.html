    After the order form is signed, you send the building-plan PDF. We confirm every sheet is there and readable, note the drawing reference, date and approval status, and ask the owner for a short written go-ahead. We never publish the drawings.

  3. Facts out of the sheets

    • 1,835 facts on Demonstration project

    We extract dimensions and schedules with sheet references, then re-check buyer-facing facts. Where sheets disagree, we record it and ask.

  4. The to-scale model

    • 103/103 checks passed

    We build the model in metres from those facts, then check it against the drawings: 20 of 20 random dimensions within 0.1 m; all 103 checks passed (101 dimensions, 2 counts). It flagged 5 items that need a site visit.

Your input and handover checklist

Which pieces come with each package is on the packages page.

You send

  1. The drawings

    After an order form is signed, the building-plan set as a PDF, all sheets. A scan works if it is legible. Tell us their approval status: the approval reference and date, or that they are only submitted.

  2. The owner's go-ahead

    A short written authority from the registered owner, and confirmation that we may use the drawings to make the showroom. We send you the wording.

  3. What to show

    Which operation-ready preset suits the buyers you want, and anything the drawings do not cover that the owner can confirm, such as power supply or floor loading.

You get

  • 3D

    Walk-in model

    To scale, in the browser, nothing to install: as per plan and one operation-ready preset; tap-to-measure to 0.1 m (not a survey). The 3D model works better on a desktop; on a phone it can take 10–15 seconds to become interactive.

  • PDF

    Spec sheet

    Areas, heights, doors, parking and finishes, each with its sheet reference. Where the drawings are silent, it says "owner to confirm".

  • Link

    Share page

    One page for the listing with a WhatsApp preview card, the agency's particulars and clear labels on every visual.

  • Showroom

    Stills and video

    Path-traced stills with as-per-plan partners and a 20–30 s vertical video. Film add-on: a 45–60 s walkthrough and 3 vertical cuts of 15 s.

  • Not offered yet

    Building-facts assistant

    Answers building questions from the drawings, citing the sheet, in English, Bahasa Melayu and 中文. It is a pilot and not part of any package yet: 80% correct on 50 unseen questions against a 95% target, with no invented figures and no price talk.

Limits

What a showroom is not

  • Not as-built and not a survey. The model follows the drawings. For existing buildings, what was built can differ from the drawings. We're setting up an on-site check of key dimensions; until then, ask us before ordering.
  • Not an approval. We say which drawing set we used, its date and its approval status. Our check is of our model against those drawings, and is not a certification by any authority, architect or engineer.
  • Not a promise about use. Operation-ready presets are illustrative. Whether a use is allowed depends on the land title and planning approval.
  • Not an estate agency. We prepare showrooms. Registered estate agents and owners market the property.
