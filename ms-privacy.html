Privacy notice · Notis privasi

Notis ini menerangkan data peribadi yang dikendalikan oleh Idyra, sebabnya, dan pilihan anda.

Dikemas kini pada 8 Okt 2026.

English

Who we are

The proprietor of Idyra (IDYRA DIGITALS STUDIO, SSM business registration no. 202603265130 (RA0140172-M)), a sole proprietorship with its registered business address in Kuala Lumpur, Malaysia, is the data controller. For questions, requests or complaints: privacy@idyra.ai or WhatsApp +60 12-250 5150. The proprietor's name is given to any data subject who asks.

What we collect

  • On this website: no analytics of our own. The partner application section below explains the separate application form. The quote-request builder prepares a draft locally in your browser. Opening WhatsApp passes that draft, including your selected property details and any town or area you enter, to WhatsApp. You choose whether to send it to us. The web host (GitHub Pages) keeps standard access logs, such as IP address and time.
  • On WhatsApp: your name and number as shown in WhatsApp, and the property details you choose to send us. Please don't send drawings before an order form is signed.
  • By email: your email address, your name if you include it, and the property details you choose to send us. Please don't send drawings before an order form is signed.
  • In drawings: after an order form is signed, title blocks in the drawings can name owners, applicants and consultants. We use them only to confirm who may authorise the work, and never publish them.
  • From others: an agent or owner may send us property details that name you. After an order form is signed, they may also provide drawings with the required written permission. We use them only for the showroom they asked for.

Your EN/BM language preference is stored only in this browser until you change it or clear site data. Questions entered in Idyra Help are matched to prepared answers in your browser; they are not saved or sent to us or an AI provider. Its WhatsApp button opens a prepared introduction, without copying your question. You choose what to send in WhatsApp.

For partner-programme interest, you may share your work focus, preferred language and the kinds of spaces in your network, alongside your name and contact details. We use these to reply and discuss suitability; this does not enrol you or create a public profile. Please introduce another person only with their permission.

Optional demo configurations are saved only in your browser or exported as files when you choose. Use Reset in the demo or clear site data to remove saved device choices. Importing a configuration reads the selected file locally; it is not uploaded to us.

Why

We use enquiry details to reply to you and prepare a quote. Drawings are requested only after an order form is signed and used to prepare your preview and carry out that order. We also keep business records the law requires. We do not sell personal data or use it for unrelated marketing.

Who else sees it

WhatsApp (Meta) carries our messages and may process them outside Malaysia. Google (Workspace) hosts our email and may process it outside Malaysia, including in the United States. We use AI services to read drawings and messages and to prepare showrooms, including Anthropic (Claude) in the United States. We ask for drawings only after an order form is signed and the written licence for the drawings is in place; those drawings, including any names in their title blocks, may be processed by these services outside Malaysia, only to prepare your preview and fulfil that signed order. Image services receive renders and models only. We share data with others only with your consent or where the law requires.

How long

Enquiries that do not lead to an order: deleted after 12 months. Order records: kept for 7 years for accounting. Drawings: deleted on request, or 90 days after hosting ends.

Your choices

You may ask to see or correct your personal data, withdraw consent, or ask us to stop messaging you, by email to privacy@idyra.ai or by WhatsApp to +60 12-250 5150. Giving us your details is voluntary; without them we cannot reply or quote.

Bahasa Melayu

Siapa kami

Pemilik Idyra (IDYRA DIGITALS STUDIO, No. pendaftaran perniagaan SSM 202603265130 (RA0140172-M)), sebuah perniagaan milikan tunggal yang beralamat perniagaan berdaftar di Kuala Lumpur, Malaysia, ialah pengawal data. Untuk pertanyaan, permintaan atau aduan: privacy@idyra.ai atau WhatsApp +60 12-250 5150. Nama pemilik diberikan kepada mana-mana subjek data yang memintanya.

Apa yang kami kumpul

  • Di laman web ini: tiada analitik kami sendiri. Bahagian permohonan rakan di bawah menerangkan borang permohonan yang berasingan. Pembina permintaan sebut harga menyediakan draf secara setempat dalam pelayar anda. Apabila anda membuka WhatsApp, draf itu, termasuk butiran hartanah yang dipilih dan bandar atau kawasan yang anda masukkan, dihantar kepada WhatsApp. Anda memilih sama ada hendak menghantarnya kepada kami. Hos laman web (GitHub Pages) menyimpan log akses biasa, seperti alamat IP dan masa.
  • Di WhatsApp: nama dan nombor anda seperti yang dipaparkan dalam WhatsApp, dan butiran hartanah yang anda pilih untuk dihantar. Jangan hantar lukisan sebelum borang pesanan ditandatangani.
  • Melalui e-mel: alamat e-mel anda, nama anda jika anda menyertakannya, dan butiran hartanah yang anda pilih untuk dihantar. Jangan hantar lukisan sebelum borang pesanan ditandatangani.
  • Dalam lukisan: selepas borang pesanan ditandatangani, blok tajuk dalam lukisan mungkin menamakan pemilik, pemohon dan perunding. Kami menggunakannya hanya untuk mengesahkan siapa yang boleh memberi kebenaran, dan tidak sekali-kali menerbitkannya.
  • Daripada pihak lain: ejen atau pemilik mungkin menghantar kepada kami butiran hartanah yang menamakan anda. Selepas borang pesanan ditandatangani, mereka juga boleh memberikan lukisan dengan kebenaran bertulis yang diperlukan. Kami menggunakannya hanya untuk showroom yang diminta.

Pilihan bahasa EN/BM disimpan hanya dalam pelayar ini sehingga anda menukarnya atau memadam data laman. Soalan dalam Bantuan Idyra dipadankan dengan jawapan yang disediakan dalam pelayar anda; soalan itu tidak disimpan atau dihantar kepada kami atau penyedia AI. Butang WhatsApp membuka mesej pengenalan yang disediakan tanpa menyalin soalan anda. Anda memilih apa yang hendak dihantar melalui WhatsApp.

Untuk minat terhadap program rakan, anda boleh berkongsi bidang kerja, bahasa pilihan dan jenis ruang dalam rangkaian anda, bersama nama dan butiran hubungan. Kami menggunakannya untuk membalas dan membincangkan kesesuaian; ini tidak mendaftarkan anda atau mewujudkan profil awam. Perkenalkan orang lain hanya dengan kebenaran mereka.

Konfigurasi demo pilihan disimpan hanya dalam pelayar anda atau dieksport sebagai fail apabila anda memilihnya. Gunakan Tetap semula dalam demo atau padam data laman untuk membuang pilihan yang disimpan pada peranti. Import konfigurasi membaca fail yang dipilih secara setempat; fail itu tidak dimuat naik kepada kami.

Mengapa

Kami menggunakan butiran pertanyaan untuk membalas anda dan menyediakan sebut harga. Lukisan hanya diminta selepas borang pesanan ditandatangani dan digunakan untuk menyediakan pratonton serta melaksanakan pesanan tersebut. Kami juga menyimpan rekod perniagaan yang dikehendaki undang-undang. Kami tidak menjual data peribadi atau menggunakannya untuk pemasaran yang tidak berkaitan.

Siapa lagi yang melihatnya

WhatsApp (Meta) membawa mesej kami dan mungkin memprosesnya di luar Malaysia. Google (Workspace) mengehoskan e-mel kami dan mungkin memprosesnya di luar Malaysia, termasuk di Amerika Syarikat. Kami menggunakan perkhidmatan AI untuk membaca lukisan dan mesej serta menyediakan showroom, termasuk Anthropic (Claude) di Amerika Syarikat. Kami hanya meminta lukisan selepas borang pesanan ditandatangani dan lesen bertulis untuk lukisan tersebut tersedia; lukisan itu, termasuk nama dalam blok tajuknya, mungkin diproses oleh perkhidmatan ini di luar Malaysia, hanya untuk menyediakan pratonton dan melaksanakan pesanan yang ditandatangani itu. Perkhidmatan imej hanya menerima render dan model. Kami berkongsi data dengan pihak lain hanya dengan persetujuan anda atau jika dikehendaki undang-undang.

Berapa lama

Pertanyaan yang tidak menjadi pesanan: dipadam selepas 12 bulan. Rekod pesanan: disimpan selama 7 tahun untuk perakaunan. Lukisan: dipadam atas permintaan, atau 90 hari selepas pengehosan tamat.

Pilihan anda

Anda boleh meminta untuk melihat atau membetulkan data peribadi anda, menarik balik persetujuan, atau meminta kami berhenti menghantar mesej, melalui e-mel ke privacy@idyra.ai atau WhatsApp ke +60 12-250 5150. Memberi butiran anda adalah secara sukarela; tanpanya kami tidak dapat membalas atau memberi sebut harga.

Partner applications · Permohonan rakan

English

This notice applies to the partner-interest form, version partner-2026-10-08. The business and contact details above apply.

The form asks for your name, email, optional telephone number, city or area, work interests, preferred language, optional introduction and permission to contact you. Do not include identity documents, bank details or other people’s personal information.

WhatsApp preparation mode: your entries stay in this page until you open WhatsApp. Opening that link passes the prepared message to WhatsApp (Meta), which may process it outside Malaysia. Idyra receives it only when you send it. Unsuccessful enquiries follow the 12-month retention period above.

Private website submission mode, when enabled: the form clearly offers a secure submission and displays a reference only after saving. Idyra uses your details to assess your interest and contact you about the programme. Authorised reviewers see them privately; no public profile, employment, appointment or commission agreement is created.

In website submission mode, Cloudflare Workers and D1 process and store the application, and Cloudflare Turnstile processes the verification token, IP address and browser security signals to prevent abuse. Processing may occur outside Malaysia. The application stores a short-lived keyed IP hash for rate limiting rather than a raw IP address; provider security logs and backups may have separate retention. We do not promise Malaysia-only storage.

Website application rows expire after 90 days and are removed by the next daily cleanup, normally within one further day. If you later join the programme, a separate agreement explains the necessary partner records. We do not use this permission for unrelated marketing. You may request access, correction, withdrawal or deletion using the contact details above. Information is voluntary, but we need the required fields to review and reply.

Mod penghantaran peribadi melalui laman, apabila diaktifkan: borang menawarkan penghantaran selamat dengan jelas dan memaparkan rujukan hanya selepas maklumat disimpan.
