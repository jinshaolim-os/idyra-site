How it works

Outcome first. Then source, review, delivery. We agree what your presentation must achieve before anything is modelled.

Start creatingLearn more
  1. Outcome

    Say what it must do

    Lease the unit, launch the shop, brief a team or explain a layout. Your initial review and quotation are free.

  2. Source

    Share what you have

    After an agreed quote and signed order, share authorised building plans; the model is built from them. Photos or a phone video can help as reference. Your drawings stay private.

  3. Review

    Check a private preview

    Review your model and the revisions included in your scope. Every view carries its label: drawing-built, illustrative or AI concept.

  4. Delivery

    Receive the agreed files

    Your browser model and selected presentation files, with handover instructions. Hosting, access, term and upkeep are agreed separately.
