HonestyLabels on every visual

You always know what you are looking at

Each image, clip and model carries one of three labels. AI may add people inside masked areas, and it never touches walls, windows, openings or dimensions.

Measured

Figures and measurements computed from the model built from the drawings, shown to 0.1 m. Not a survey.

Rendered from building-plan drawings

Path-traced or 3D output with no AI pixels. "Fit-out illustrative" is added when racking, furniture or vehicles are shown.

AI-illustrative

Any image with AI-generated pixels. The people in these images are generated and do not show real individuals.

Questions

Good questions, clear answers

Is it a video, a render or something else?

All three, each labelled. The core is an interactive, to-scale 3D model that buyers control in the browser. Path-traced stills and a short film come with it, made from the same model.

Do I need the drawings?

For a Plan Twin or a Showroom, yes: the building-plan drawings as a PDF, with their approval status (the approval reference, or that they are only submitted). No drawings? Tell us on WhatsApp. We don't offer a photo-based pack yet.

How accurate is it? Is it a survey?

We check the model against the drawings it was built from: on PT 49665, 20 of 20 random dimensions came within 0.1 m, and all 103 checks passed (101 dimensions, 2 counts). It is not a survey. For existing buildings, what was built can differ from the drawings. We're setting up an on-site check of key dimensions; until then, ask us before ordering.

Does AI change my building?

No. The building comes from the drawings. AI may only add people inside masked areas of empty floor, never walls, windows, openings or dimensions, and every such image is labelled AI-illustrative.

Can buyers open it on a phone?

Yes, in the phone's browser, with nothing to install; the 3D needs a WebGL-capable browser. Stills and film load quickly. The 3D model works better on a desktop; on a phone it can take 10–15 seconds to become interactive.

Do you sell or let the property?

No. Idyra is not an estate agent. It prepares showrooms for owners, developers and registered estate agents, and does not offer property for sale or rent.

What does it cost?

A Plan Twin is RM2,900 and a Showroom starts at RM8,900, with founding prices for our first customers. Nothing is payable until an order form is signed. See the packages.

What happens to my drawings?

You keep them. We take a licence to use them for your showroom only, never publish them, and delete them on request. Privacy notice.
